Stories of the day, cheers, Runner of the week. Tap anyone, follow them on Instagram in one tap.

The game

Rankings, streaks, records. A reason to come back.

Today, this week, this month. Ranked by distance, not speed, so everyone has a shot. And a nudge when someone passes you.
